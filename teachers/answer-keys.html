Answer keys

Answer keys for teachers. The worksheet answers come from facts on our anatomy, habitats and ID guide pages.

Last reviewed: 8 October 2026

Snake adaptations worksheet

  1. Forked tongue: B
  2. Spectacle (brille): D
  3. Wide belly scales: A
  4. Heat-sensing pits: E
  5. Stretchy jaw ligament: C
  6. False. Snakes live on every continent except Antarctica.
  7. False. The lower jaw halves are joined by a stretchy ligament and the skull bones are loosely connected; the jaw does not actually unhinge.
  8. True. This is called ecdysis.
  9. True. In most snakes only the right lung works, and it is very long.
  10. Sample answer: snakes are ectotherms and depend on outside heat, so long cold periods make survival hard. The adder survives by brumating (resting) through the winter.
  11. Sample answer: predators may avoid it because it looks and sounds like a dangerous rattlesnake, so it is more likely to survive.
  12. Sample answer: many harmless snakes look like dangerous ones, colours vary, and even harmless snakes can bite. Only experts should handle snakes.
