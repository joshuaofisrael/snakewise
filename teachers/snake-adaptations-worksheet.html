Part B. True or false?

  1. Snakes live on every continent, including Antarctica.  
  2. A snake's jaw really "unhinges" when it eats.  
  3. Snakes shed the outer layer of their skin as they grow.

Part C. Habitat argument

10. The European adder lives as far north as the Arctic Circle. It survives the long, cold winter by brumating (a rest similar to hibernation). Snakes are ectotherms. Use these facts to explain why most snakes cannot survive in very cold places, and how the adder manages.

Part D. Think like a scientist

11. A gopher snake shakes its tail and hisses, but it has no rattle. Why might looking like a rattlesnake help a harmless snake survive?

12. Why should you never touch a wild snake, even if you think you know what it is?
