Quick facts

  • Snakes have no eyelids; a clear scale called the spectacle (brille) protects each eye.
  • They smell with their forked tongue and the Jacobson's (vomeronasal) organ in the roof of the mouth.
  • Most snakes are harmless to people; only a minority of species have medically significant venom.
  • Snakes shed their whole skin, often in one piece, as they grow.
