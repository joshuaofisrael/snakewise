Snake identification and safety

Snakes vary a lot in colour and pattern, harmless species often mimic dangerous ones, and our identification guide is simplified and is not a field key. Never handle, approach, catch or kill a wild snake based on anything on this site, and never assume a snake is harmless because it looks like a "harmless" example here. Keep your distance and contact local wildlife or snake-removal professionals. If a bite may have happened, call your local emergency number or a poison control centre straight away.
