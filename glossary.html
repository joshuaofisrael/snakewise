Snake glossary

Plain-English definitions of common snake and herpetology terms, from brumation and ecdysis to elapid, scute and viviparous.

Jump to: Brumation · Colubrid · Constriction · Ecdysis · Ectotherm · Elapid · Fang · Heat pit · Herpetology · Jacobson's organ · Neurotoxin · Oviparous · Viviparous · Ophiophagous · Scute · Spectacle · Venom · Viper
Brumation
Reptile dormancy during cold periods, similar to hibernation.
Colubrid
Member of Colubridae, the largest snake family; most are harmless.
Constriction
Subduing prey by coiling and squeezing.
Ecdysis
Shedding of the outer skin layer.
Ectotherm
Animal relying on external heat to regulate body temperature.
Elapid
Front-fanged venomous snakes such as cobras, mambas, sea snakes and coral snakes.
Fang
Specialised tooth for delivering venom.
Heat pit
Organ that senses infrared heat, found in pit vipers, boas and pythons.
Herpetology
The study of reptiles and amphibians.
Jacobson's organ
Vomeronasal organ in the roof of the mouth used for smell-taste.
Neurotoxin
Toxin affecting the nervous system.
Oviparous
Laying eggs.
Viviparous
Giving birth to live young.
Ophiophagous
Eating other snakes.
Scute
Large scale, especially the broad belly scales.
Spectacle (brille)
Transparent scale covering the eye.
Venom
Toxic secretion injected by bite.
Viper
Member of Viperidae with long hinged fangs.
