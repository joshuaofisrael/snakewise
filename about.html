About SnakeWise

SnakeWise is a free, plain-language guide for anyone fascinated by snakes, covering how snakes work, notable species, safety and myths, plus a region-by-region ID guide and an original game. SnakeWise is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What SnakeWise is

SnakeWise explains snakes for curious beginners and enthusiasts: anatomy, movement, venom and constriction, notable species, habitats, myths, safety and pet basics. We also publish a venomous-or-not ID guide, fact articles on the blog, and The Slither Game. A shop for snake gear such as plush snakes is coming soon.

How we source facts

  • Blog articles are based on peer-reviewed studies and reputable references such as the Reptile Database, and every article lists its sources with links.
  • We do not make up facts or citations. If a claim cannot be checked against a source, we leave it out.
  • Our text, illustrations and game are original.
  • Corrections are welcome: email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.
