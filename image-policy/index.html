Image policy

This page explains how SnakeWise uses images: which photos are AI-generated illustrations, which are real licensed wildlife photographs, and where each photo's Source link leads.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

AI-generated photographs

Many of the photo-style images on SnakeWise are AI-generated. They are not photographs of real snakes, people or places; they are illustrative images made for this site with an image-generation tool and contain no third-party photos. Each one is captioned "AI-generated image for illustration, not for identification," and that caption's Source link leads to this page. Never use an AI-generated image to identify a snake in the wild. For identification, use our venomous-or-not ID guide and the real photographs credited below, and if you are ever unsure, treat the snake as venomous and keep your distance (see our safety page).

Real, licensed wildlife photographs

SnakeWise also uses real photographs of snakes. Each one is used under a free licence that allows reuse — Creative Commons CC0, Creative Commons CC BY, or public domain — and most come from Wikimedia Commons or Flickr. Every real photo is individually credited on our photo credits page with its photographer, source link, licence and any changes we made, such as resizing or converting the file to WebP. We do not use photographs of identifiable people or logos, and we do not alter a real photograph's markings or colours in a way that could misrepresent the species shown.

Original diagrams

A few blog posts also include simple line diagrams drawn in-house, for example to show jaw mechanics or how a snake sheds its skin. These are original diagrams, not photographs and not AI-generated images; each is captioned as a diagram, and where one is drawn to a specific measurement from a study, the caption names that source.

Credits and questions

For the specific photographer, licence or source behind any single image, see our credits page, which lists every real photo and every AI-generated illustration currently in use on the site. If you are a rights holder and believe a credit is wrong or missing, or you have any other question about an image, please use our contact form and we will fix or remove it promptly.
