Tap or click one tile, then another, to swap them. Keyboard: Tab to a tile and press Enter.

What this game teaches

  • Snakes shed the outer layer of their skin as they grow; the process is called ecdysis.
  • Before shedding, a snake's eyes often look cloudy blue.
  • Snakes have no eyelids: a clear scale called the spectacle (brille) protects each eye.
  • A snake often sheds its whole skin in one piece.
