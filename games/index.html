Snake games

Play free, original snake games that teach real facts. Every game works on phones and computers with touch, mouse or keyboard, has no sound, ads or sign-up, and keeps your best score only in your own browser.

Lookalike Lab

Read the clues and guess: venomous or harmless? Learn the features our ID guide uses in six regions. For learning only, not safety advice.

Scale Snap

A memory match of snake markings: zigzags, hourglass bands, chevrons and more. Each pair reveals whose pattern it is.

Shed Shuffle

Swap tiles to rebuild a snake's shed-day picture, then learn how snakes shed their skin.

The Slither Game

Our original arcade game: eat the mice, grow longer and don't bump into the walls or yourself.
